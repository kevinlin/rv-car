Why the 500 MAX, for two adults living aboard

  • 48 V at 6 kW. Same stored energy either way, but a modern voltage platform: 125 A through the inverter at full load where 12 V needs 250 A for half the output.
  • A 5 kg washer-dryer. Two people’s clothes, jeans, a thin sheet. The 3 kg alternative does underwear and t-shirts.
  • 2200 × 1400 mm up front. Published, and enough. The Z76’s alcove size is nowhere on record.
  • Three adjustable chairs rather than a booth held upright to seat six people you are not hosting.

Where the Z76 still wins

  • 110 L of grey against 75 L. Forty-seven per cent more, and off-grid it is usually grey that fills first, not fresh that runs out.
  • A galley specified for actually cooking: gas hob, high-output extraction, a window over the sink, and a door that shuts on all of it.
  • Eberspächer 5 kW, named and rated, doing hot water and cabin heat in one box. The 500 MAX’s hot-water source is not published at all.
  • Published where it counts: overall size, slide travel, fridge zones. Fewer blanks to chase down.

Before signing anything

Six things the spec sheet does not answer

None of these are on any published page for the 500 MAX. Get them in writing rather than off a show vehicle.

  1. Washroom internal clear size, length × width × height.
  2. The hot-water heater: brand, model, output.
  3. What the hob actually is: induction, diesel or gas, and its rating.
  4. Whether 75 L of grey can be built out to 100–120 L from the factory.
  5. Whether the manual jacks can be ordered as four-point electric or hydraulic auto-levelling.
  6. Kerb weight as delivered, axle loads with the tanks full, and what payload is left. 270 L of fresh water is 270 kg before the battery, the appliances, two people and the luggage.

The model is the argument

Layout, finishes and camera stops all live in data with a confidence tag attached, and a dimensional check runs at startup: no furniture overlaps, everything inside its volume, aisle at least 400 mm, both published bed sizes exact. Go and look.
